Cybersecurity Interview Preparation
What is the CIA triad in cybersecurity?

Answer: The CIA triad stands for Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability. These are the three fundamental principles of information security that guide the development of security policies and procedures.

How would you handle a security incident?

Answer: I would follow the incident response process: 1) Identification and assessment, 2) Containment to prevent spread, 3) Eradication of the threat, 4) Recovery and restoration, 5) Lessons learned and documentation.

What's the difference between vulnerability and threat?

Answer: A vulnerability is a weakness in a system that could be exploited, while a threat is a potential danger that could exploit that vulnerability. Risk is the likelihood that a threat will exploit a vulnerability.

Explain the concept of defense in depth.

Answer: Defense in depth is a layered security approach that uses multiple security controls at different levels to protect against various attack vectors. If one layer fails, others continue to provide protection.
