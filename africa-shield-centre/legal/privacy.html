Privacy

What this site keeps.

Incident notes, contact drafts, and event interest are stored in your browser’s local storage. They are not uploaded with the public site. Clearing the site data in the browser deletes them. Export a copy first if you need one.

The threat desk downloads a snapshot that this project refreshes from CISA, abuse.ch, and the regional internet registries. That file contains public catalog entries and public indicators. It does not contain your notes.

The centre’s own phone numbers and the address in Nyarutarama are published contact details. Do not put a password or a full customer list into the notebook.

A self-hosted copy can be wired to the PHP services in this repository. Those services are not part of the public website, and this page does not send your password to them.
