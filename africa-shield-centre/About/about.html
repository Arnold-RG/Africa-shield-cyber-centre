OUR JOURNEY

From vision to reality - building East Africa's cyber defense

2020 - The Vision

Recognizing the growing cyber threats facing East Africa, a group of cybersecurity experts and technology leaders came together with a shared vision: to create a regional cybersecurity powerhouse that could protect millions of people and critical infrastructure from sophisticated cyber attacks.

2022 - Foundation & Launch

Africa Shield Cyber Centre was officially established with backing from leading technology companies, government partnerships, and international cybersecurity organizations. Our first Security Operations Center (SOC) went live, immediately beginning to monitor and protect critical infrastructure across Kenya, Uganda, and Tanzania.

2023 - Rapid Expansion

Our services expanded to cover 14 East African countries, establishing regional partnerships and deploying advanced AI-powered threat detection systems. We launched our comprehensive cybersecurity training programs and began protecting over 100 million people across the region.

2024 - Recognition & Innovation

Africa Shield received international recognition for cybersecurity excellence, winning multiple awards for innovation in AI-powered threat detection. We launched our quantum-resistant encryption research program and established partnerships with leading global cybersecurity firms.

2025 - Continental Leadership

Today, we protect over 400 million people across East Africa, operate multiple state-of-the-art SOCs, and lead the continent in cybersecurity innovation. Our vision for a secure, digitally sovereign Africa is becoming reality as we continue to expand our capabilities and reach.
