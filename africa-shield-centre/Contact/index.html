Frequently Asked Questions

For critical cybersecurity incidents, our emergency response team responds within 15 minutes. For general support inquiries, we respond within 2 hours during business hours and within 24 hours for non-urgent matters. Our 24/7 emergency hotline ensures immediate assistance for critical threats.

We provide support in English, Swahili, French, and Amharic. Our multilingual team ensures effective communication across East Africa. For specialized technical support, we can also arrange translation services for other local languages.

Yes, we offer on-site consultations for enterprise clients across East Africa. Our certified cybersecurity experts can visit your location for security assessments, incident response, training, and implementation of security solutions. Contact our business team to schedule a consultation.

You can report threats through multiple channels: our emergency hotline (+250 788 911 911), USSD code (*789*911#), email (emergency@africashield.org), or through our secure reporting portal. For immediate threats, always use the emergency hotline for fastest response.

We offer comprehensive cybersecurity training including: Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH), CompTIA Security+, CISSP preparation, mobile money security, incident response, and custom corporate training. Programs are available online, in-person, and in hybrid formats across East Africa.

We welcome partnerships with organizations across East Africa. Contact our partnerships team at partnerships@africashield.org or call +250 788 789 789. We offer various partnership models including technology integration, reseller programs, and regional representation opportunities.

We provide services across Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda, Ethiopia, Burundi, and South Sudan. Our headquarters is in Kigali, Rwanda, with regional offices in major cities. Remote support is available throughout East Africa, and on-site services can be arranged in all covered countries.

Yes, we offer free basic cybersecurity assessments for small businesses and NGOs in East Africa. This includes a preliminary security review and recommendations. For comprehensive assessments and penetration testing, we offer competitive rates with detailed reporting and remediation guidance.
