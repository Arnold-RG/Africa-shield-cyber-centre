Questions already asked

How fast is an incident answered?

Critical calls were described at 15 minutes. General questions, within two hours in the working day, and within a day otherwise.

Which languages?

English, Kiswahili, French, and Amharic. The site navigation also offers Kinyarwanda.

Do you visit?

Yes, for institutions in the region: assessments, response, and training.

How do I report a threat?

Phone +250 788 911 911, USSD *789*911#, or the notebook on the report page. The phone is the fast path.

What training exists?

Ethical hacking preparation, Security+, CISSP preparation, mobile money, incident response, and courses written for a specific organisation.

Can a small organisation get a first look for free?

The centre has offered a short assessment to small businesses and NGOs. A full test is paid work.
